## What Is the Analytics-Con-202 Salesforce Certification?

Analytics-Con-202 is the exam for the Salesforce Certified Tableau Next Consultant credential. It is designed for professionals who help organizations turn data into actionable insights through Salesforce analytics technologies, semantic models, and AI-driven capabilities.

The certification focuses on more than creating dashboards. Candidates should understand how data is prepared and modeled, how Analytics Agents support business users, how Tableau Next integrates with Salesforce platforms, and how analytics assets are managed and shared across organizations.

## Who Should Take the Analytics-Con-202 Exam?

This certification is suitable for data analysts, business intelligence consultants, Salesforce consultants, analytics specialists, and solution architects who work with enterprise data and reporting solutions.

Candidates should ideally have practical experience with Salesforce Setup, user permissions, Data Cloud (Data 360), data integration, and analytics workflows. Familiarity with generative AI concepts and agent-based experiences is also valuable, particularly when configuring analytics capabilities that help users explore data and generate insights.

## Analytics-Con-202 Exam Objectives and Weighting

The exam covers six major knowledge areas. Understanding their relative weights can help candidates prioritize their study time and identify topics that deserve additional practice.

**Data Setup — 20%**

This domain covers semantic model components, design, and application, along with Data 360 capabilities for data integration, management, preparation, and modeling. Candidates should be able to evaluate a scenario and determine an appropriate data architecture or processing approach.

**Agentic Experiences — 25%**

As the largest exam domain, this section focuses on generative AI capabilities within Tableau Next. Candidates should understand how Analytics Agents help users explore data, generate insights, and receive proactive alerts. They should also know how to assess agent readiness and support the creation of Tableau Next components.

**Embedding, Cross-Cloud, and Interoperability — 20%**

This domain examines how Tableau Next integrates with Salesforce analytics platforms and how analytics assets appear within business workflows. Candidates should understand embedding options, Tableau Next Apps, relevant developer tools and APIs, and the role of marketplace offerings in delivering business value.

**Basic Setup and Administration — 10%**

This section focuses on activating and managing Tableau Next features, configuring appropriate user access, and enabling Agentic Analytics for users. A solid understanding of Salesforce administration and permission management provides a useful foundation.

**Visualizations and Dashboards — 15%**

Candidates should understand visualization and dashboard design principles for creating clear, user-friendly analytics experiences. They should also know how to configure actions within Tableau Next to support meaningful user interactions.

**Managing Workspaces and Organizations — 10%**

This domain covers deploying and managing a Personal Org, sharing and managing Tableau Next assets, and understanding how Tableau Next assets are deployed. Candidates should be familiar with workspace organization and the controls that support effective collaboration.
